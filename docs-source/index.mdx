DSOM — Deep State of Mind — is a metacognitive governance framework that transforms any AI (Gemini, Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT) into a disciplined **Cognitive Digital Twin**. By storing your project's entire working context in Git, DSOM ensures your AI never loses its memory between sessions, never executes without your approval, and never locks you into a single provider.

## Why DSOM?

Every AI chat session starts with amnesia. DSOM solves that by making your Git repository the **Single Source of Truth** for all AI context — so switching models, closing a tab, or starting a new session never loses a single decision.

## Get Started in Minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clone the DSOM skeleton">
    Fork or clone the repository to use as a template for your new project, then run the reset script to clear the old history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initialise the Brain">
    Run the `init-brain` script for your platform to create your `.agents/brain/` directory with the three core artifacts: `task.md`, `walkthrough.md`, and `implementation_plan.md`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your Project Identity Card">
    Open `docs/AI-COGNITIVE-TWIN-PROTOCOL.md` and complete every field with your real project details — this is the most critical step and what gives the AI its full project context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Perform the Cognitive Handshake">
    Run `bash tools/reanimate.sh` to generate your context manifest, then upload it to your AI and say the Handshake phrase to activate your Cognitive Digital Twin.
  </Step>
</Steps>
